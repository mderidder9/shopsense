ShopSense · Mockup v1
At the counter, with a second opinion.
Scenario: 2017 Accord, 68,420 mi, a $965 dealer quote. Screens 01–10 are one connected flow — sign up and add a car (01–04), snap, upload or type a quote (05–06), and approve/decline on 07 updates savings, shop estimates and history live.
Assumptions: estimates come from user-uploaded invoices (report count shown for trust); "upsell rate" = share of a shop's quotes containing not-due items. Mobile-first, since the decision happens standing at the service desk.
