ShopSense · Mockup v1
At the counter, with a second opinion.
Scenario: 2017 Accord, 68,420 mi, a $965 dealer quote. Screens 00–10 are one connected flow — sign in or sign up and add a car (00–04; accounts and garages are saved in the local database), snap, upload or type a quote (05–06), and approve/decline on 07 updates savings, shop estimates and history live. Real AI: screen 04b (what's due + which shop), which also fills "Due now" on 04 and the AI picks on 08. Everything else is simulated. Open AI log →
Assumptions: estimates come from user-uploaded invoices (report count shown for trust); "upsell rate" = share of a shop's quotes containing not-due items. Mobile-first, since the decision happens standing at the service desk.
