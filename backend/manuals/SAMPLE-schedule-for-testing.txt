Vehicle family: mid-size sedan, 2.4L 4-cylinder, model years 2016-2018

SECTION 6 - MAINTENANCE  (page 6-1)

6.1 How to use this schedule (page 6-1)
Follow the NORMAL schedule unless the vehicle is driven mainly in any of
these SEVERE conditions: trips under 5 miles, extreme heat or cold,
towing, dusty or unpaved roads, extended idling. Under SEVERE conditions
use the shorter interval shown in brackets [ ].
Do each service at the mileage OR time shown, whichever comes first.

6.2 Scheduled maintenance (page 6-2)

  Engine oil and filter (synthetic 0W-20)
      Every 7,500 mi or 12 months   [severe: every 5,000 mi or 6 months]

  Tire rotation
      Every 7,500 mi

  Inspect brakes (pads, rotors, lines), suspension, steering, exhaust,
  fluid levels, lights, wipers, belts and hoses
      Every 15,000 mi or 12 months

  Cabin air (dust and pollen) filter
      Every 15,000 mi                [severe: every 7,500 mi]

  Engine air filter
      Every 30,000 mi                [severe: every 15,000 mi]

  Brake fluid
      Every 3 years, regardless of mileage

  Continuously variable transmission (CVT) fluid - drain and refill
      Every 30,000 mi                [severe: every 25,000 mi]
      NOTE: This vehicle requires a drain-and-refill. A pressure
      "flush" of the transmission is NOT recommended.

  Spark plugs (iridium)
      Every 105,000 mi

  Valve clearance - inspect and adjust if noisy
      Every 105,000 mi

  Engine coolant
      First at 120,000 mi or 10 years, then every 60,000 mi or 5 years

  Drive belt - inspect
      Every 60,000 mi

6.3 Items NOT on the schedule (page 6-4)
The following are not required maintenance for this engine and are only
needed if a specific problem is diagnosed: fuel injection / fuel system
cleaning, engine "decarbonizing", power steering flush (electric power
steering - no fluid).

6.4 Wear items (page 6-4)
Brake pads, tires, wiper blades and the 12V battery wear at different
rates. Replace based on inspection, not mileage:
  - Brake pads: replace below 3 mm (0.12 in) of pad material.
  - Tires: replace below 2/32 in tread depth.
  - Battery: replace if it fails a load test.
